Accommodation Display

Controls the discreet, anonymous prayer-window indicator shown on the ORB and SCRAP•E Legacy displays. When on, students who observe daily prayer will see a small "Prayer Window" indicator during the Dhuhr (and, if applicable, Asr) window each day — automatically, with no student names or identifying information. This is separate from the school's 10-minute pass policy, which teachers manage directly.

Calculation Method

Islamic prayer times are computed from the sun's position, not a fixed clock time. Different communities use slightly different calculation conventions — this mainly affects when Asr begins. If you're unsure, MoonsightingCommittee + Shafi is a common default for North America.
